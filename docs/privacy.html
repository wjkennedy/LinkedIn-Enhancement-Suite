Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 17, 2026

LinkedIn Enhancement Suite (LES) works in your browser to apply the feed preferences you choose.

LES does not sell, rent, or use your information for advertising.
What LES handles

Data stays in your browser.

LinkedIn page content

LES reads the LinkedIn pages where it runs so it can identify posts and apply your selected labels, filters, and display preferences. This processing happens locally in your browser.

Your settings

LES stores your extension settings, filter rules, and related local state in your browser so your preferences persist between sessions.

Optional actions

If you choose an optional feature that opens a link, downloads content, or connects to a service, LES uses the information necessary to complete that action. Such features require your interaction.

How information is used

Only to provide LES.

LES uses the information it handles only to provide its user-facing features. It does not transmit LinkedIn page content, your filter rules, or your local extension data to LES developers or to advertising networks.

Your choices
ChangeEdit settings at any time
RemoveClear browser extension data
UninstallRemove LES from your browser

Browser controls let you manage or delete locally stored extension data.

Chrome Web Store Limited Use

LES uses information obtained through Chrome permissions only to provide its LinkedIn feed controls. LES does not transfer that information to third parties, use it for advertising, or allow people to read it. This use complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
Sharing, retention, and contact

We do not share your data.

LES has no account system and does not operate a service that receives your extension data. Local data remains until you change it, clear it through your browser, or uninstall the extension. If an optional user-requested action opens a third-party service, that service handles information under its own policies. We may update this policy as LES evolves; the effective date above will be updated when we do.

Questions about this policy can be submitted through the project's GitHub issue tracker.
